Budget Explorer

Interactive charts built from Brookline's published financial data. All figures come from official town sources listed below each chart.

Revenue and Expenditure Trends

Total town revenue and total expenditures, FY2025 through FY2030. Revenue figures include property taxes, local receipts, state aid, and other sources. Expenditure figures include all town and school spending. Where lines diverge, the town is projected to spend more than it takes in.

Source: FY2025 Financial Plan, FY2026-FY2030 Long Range Financial Plan (Town of Brookline)

Where Revenue Comes From

Brookline's revenue broken down by source over ten fiscal years. Property taxes make up the largest share. "Free cash" is unspent money from prior years that gets certified for use in the next budget.

Source: FY2025 Financial Plan, FY2026-FY2030 Long Range Financial Plan (Town of Brookline)

Where the Money Goes

Major spending categories from FY2025 through FY2030. The School Department and employee benefits are the two largest categories. "Non-Appropriated" covers items like state assessments and overlay reserve that don't go through the normal budget vote.

Source: FY2025 Financial Plan, FY2026-FY2030 Long Range Financial Plan (Town of Brookline)

Projected Cumulative Deficit

If current revenue and spending trends continue, this shows the projected gap between what the town takes in and what it spends, broken down between Town-side and School-side shortfalls. These projections are from the Long Range Financial Plan.

Source: FY2026-FY2030 Long Range Financial Plan (Town of Brookline)

Benefits Cost Breakdown

The major components of employee benefits spending. Group health insurance and pensions are the largest pieces. "OPEB" stands for Other Post-Employment Benefits, mainly retiree healthcare. These costs are for both town and school employees combined.

Source: FY2025 Financial Plan, FY2026-FY2030 Long Range Financial Plan (Town of Brookline)

Revenue vs. Expenditure Growth Rates

Year-over-year percentage change in total revenue compared to total expenditures. When the expenditure bar is taller than the revenue bar, spending is growing faster than income for that year.

Source: FY2026-FY2030 Long Range Financial Plan (Town of Brookline)

How the FY2025 Budget Is Divided

A snapshot of how the FY2025 adopted budget is allocated by function. Education (including school employee benefits) accounts for the majority of spending.

Source: FY2025 Financial Plan (Town of Brookline)
